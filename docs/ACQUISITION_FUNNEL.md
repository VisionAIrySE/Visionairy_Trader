# Acquisition decision checkpoint

The user's clarified objective is a comparative capital-allocation and management assistant: progressively combine applicable intelligence to decide where, when, at what price and at what scale to acquire equity in upward momentum opportunities, typically held 1-2 days and extending up to about a week. The current objective is an opportunistic, risk-mitigating, gain-taking platform; this supersedes the earlier shorter-horizon emphasis. Stock identification is an intermediate step. Cash availability, conditional ROI, risk and evidence quality must inform comparisons against competing opportunities and retaining cash. Build on the existing SOT, hypotheses, history, schema, learning and decision models. The user's "Jarvis of DayTrading" direction requests an integrated, proactive experience, not autonomous trading authority or a profit guarantee.

## Implemented locally

`research/acquisition_funnel.py` connects to `purpose_pipeline.py`. Named evidence requirements precede cost/opportunity checks, comparative ranking and shared cash/risk reservations. Missing, failed, future and stale evidence remain distinct. Required evidence groups are explicit per experiment; all accepted indicators need not be simultaneously mandatory.

Comparisons expose conditional net reward/risk and conditional ROI at the same initial budget, a transparent lexicographic research ranking, and conditional dominance on those metrics. No invented confidence score or expected return. Correlated indicators do not earn additive points. Fixed fees are included at the previewed and final allowed scale. Financial geometry is conditional on an unvalidated target hypothesis and is not proof of positive expectancy. No optimized ranking or allocation is established.

Cash and 1% per-trade / 5% aggregate planned-risk allowances are reserved across competing candidates. Entry ceiling, size, stop, costs, conditional reward, holding horizon and reasons remain visible. Unaffordable candidates remain track-only. Duplicate opportunities cannot consume budget twice. Existing/reserved symbols need a separate addition review. The planner refuses live mode and has no order transport.

Joined research uses a conservative chronological reservation ledger without releases, settlement, mark-to-market or daily-equity pause simulation. Its independent replay outcomes are NOT basket outcomes. Future outcomes do not determine selection. Catalyst-only/combined, market/sector and other advanced intelligence families remain accepted but not connected to this implementation; the joined experiment still covers quiet-to-active price/volume only. Management fields do not implement additions, partials, protection amendments or overnight decisions.

## Allocation-to-outcome checkpoint

Continued from current SOT v0.52, including section 35. The objective remains a short-term, risk-mitigating, gain-capturing momentum assistant. Strong net ROI is the goal to demonstrate, not an established result.

Previously, `outcomes` replayed every opportunity with an independent cash/risk budget, even where the acquisition planner did not select it. Those independent controls remain intact. New `acquisition_outcomes` evaluate choices constrained by `acquisition_plan` and retain nonselected opportunities with their reasons.

Each reserved case binds exact setup/assessment fingerprints, decision time, allocation policy, cost assumptions, quantity, entry cash, planned risk, minimum net reward/risk and entry ceiling. At delayed modeled entry, replay can reduce quantity but cannot enlarge a reservation or move the structural stop. Prices above the ceiling produce nonfills. Smaller quantities must still clear the allocated reward/risk threshold after fixed fees. Changed assessment or costs require a new allocation. Outputs retain reservation and source-input fingerprints; these records grant no approval authority.

Reservations remain locked for this entire conservative experiment, including nonfills and completed exits. Later outcomes do not reallocate earlier capital; proceeds are not recycled. These are allocation-bound conditional cases, not a settled portfolio, joint equity curve or account-return calculation. Mark-to-market equity, daily pause integration, settlement, correlation, additions, partial profits and adaptive exit decisions remain incomplete.
