# Conditional targets, paper liquidity and comparison cash

The owner can choose an explicit simulation cash amount and a 2R or 3R target scenario in the private dashboard. No mandatory paper balance was agreed. Saving these settings creates an immutable comparison configuration; it does not initialize an active position ledger, fund an account, submit an order or reset positions. The next current-session test can compare competing candidates against that empty, shared-cash counterfactual scenario. Earlier reports retain their original inputs.

R is the original setup reference price minus its proposed stop. Target alternatives use the exact original setup terms and only bars completed by that setup time. Later highs cannot improve the earlier hypothesis. Both alternatives retain source identities, explicit experimental support and null expected-return/win-probability fields. Neither a risk multiple nor a favorable conditional ratio establishes likelihood of reaching the price or positive expectancy. The horizon is two regular sessions, not an adopted exit policy.

Paper liquidity requires actual fresh bid/ask timestamps and three contiguous completed five-minute bars with a fresh trailing edge. The quantity ceiling is one percent of the smallest observed share-volume bar in that window, rounded down. This is a disclosed uncalibrated participation assumption, not displayed executable depth or a fill guarantee. Quote size fields are not multiplied by an assumed lot size. Spread must clear the explicit experimental two-percent limit. Low capacity blocks sizing without removing the opportunity from discovery.

The connected review uses existing shared-capital sizing and one-percent per-trade/five-percent aggregate planned-risk ceilings. It checks required momentum, liquidity and open regular-session evidence before a conditional paper reservation. It models 0.2 percent slippage per side and zero fixed fees as uncalibrated research inputs. Missing/failed/stale evidence remains visible. A configured comparison budget is not reconciled broker capital or a live paper-position ledger.

Budget input is explicit, positive USD with at most two decimal places and bounded to one million dollars; target choices are restricted to the displayed hypotheses. Private HTTP authentication and the explicit paper-review request header are required. Stored identity, authority mode and timestamps are checked before using a setting. No automatic settings migration or cash inference is performed.

Local validation: 246 research/dashboard tests plus 7 sizing tests, including a positive synthetic open-session case that connects setup, selected budget, target, liquidity cap and shared risk into a conditional allocation; future-bar invariance, stale quotes, unknown size units, invalid/mutated cash configurations, private routes and negative cases remain covered. Hosted validation must distinguish empty owner configuration from a chosen setting; synthetic test cash is not written to the owner's configuration.

Remaining: representative target/strategy evaluation, calibrated execution evidence, active paper ledger and approval/position lifecycle, broader live strategy coverage, source-grounded model/news context and notification delivery. Qualified recommendations and unattended operation remain off.

Primary field documentation: https://docs.tradier.com/market-data/get-quotes and https://docs.tradier.com/docs/timesale. The cited quote schema lists size and lot-size fields; it does not by itself establish usable executable depth for this application.
