# Candidate qualification and comparison

The platform must compare opportunities with each other and available capital. A price/volume observation alone is not a buy recommendation.

## Implemented path

Independent breakout detection now runs without a quiet-stock prerequisite. It uses explicitly versioned completed-bar structure, unusual recent bar volume and ATR/extension limits. The existing structural classifier then tracks a controlled pullback and resumed move under a distinct independent-momentum cohort. Quiet-to-active discovery remains separate. Each completed window retains its activation, nonactivation or insufficient-evidence reason. Future/uncompleted bars are removed before baselines and validation; missing bars are never imputed. The checked-in independent_experiment.json freezes an exploratory detection configuration, not approved trading rules or a complete entry/exit strategy specification.

candidate_review.py connects exact setup terms to the existing remaining-opportunity and acquisition planner. Fresh bid/ask, a source-identified decision-time target, required evidence groups, liquidity limits and reconciled paper capital are explicit inputs. The existing planner compares conditional reward/risk and ROI at common initial capital and reserves shared cash/risk. These metrics are conditional geometry, not estimated expected return or win probability. Missing capital never becomes an invented account balance; missing evidence retains an unranked, zero-quantity review. The planner rejects live-account mode and grants no order authority.

## Captured-history diagnostic

The frozen existing coverage cohort produced 255 initial breakouts and 32 pullback continuations under the exploratory specification. The separate preferred-symbol recovery is not silently substituted into this frozen plan.

Instrument/calendar revisions, historical membership, sparse bars and corporate-action qualification remain limitations. The report retains spec, code, capture, plan and calendar identities.

The private /api/candidates endpoint serves 25 retained historical reviews per page, with source identities and reasons. It is a saved diagnostic, not a live recommendation feed. The hosted store uses authenticated immutable namespaced job rows and checks report identity on read. Existing monitor/model operations are preserved.
